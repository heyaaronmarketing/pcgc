Rent-to-own golf cart FAQ

The real questions buyers ask us — straight answers, no fine-print games.

How does rent to own a golf cart work?

Our rent-to-own program is run through HCI AppXpress, a national specialty-finance partner. You pick a cart from our lot, apply online in about 90 seconds, and get an instant pre-qualification. Approved applicants put down the first payment, we deliver the cart, and you make monthly payments until you own it — or buy it out early for a discount. We service it the whole time.

Do you need good credit to qualify?

No. The rent-to-own path exists specifically for buyers who can't get approved through traditional golf-cart loans. HCI AppXpress looks at income and ability to pay more than credit score. Many approvals happen in the 500s and even without a credit history at all.

Is there a credit check?

Yes, but it's a soft check at the pre-qualification step — it won't hurt your score. A hard pull only happens if you decide to move forward with the agreement.

How much is the down payment?

There's no traditional down payment. You pay the first monthly payment at delivery to get started — that's it. No $2,000 down like a conventional loan.

What are the monthly payments on a rent-to-own golf cart?

Payments depend on the cart you pick and the term length you choose, but most Breezy EV carts land in the $150–$350 per month range. Used carts are lower. You'll see the exact number before you sign anything.

Can I buy the cart out early?

Yes. HCI AppXpress agreements include an early buyout option. Pay off the remaining balance (minus the finance charge) at any point and the cart is yours free and clear. There's no prepayment penalty.

What happens if the cart needs service during rent-to-own?

We service it. Any mechanical issue inside normal wear-and-tear while you're still renting is on us — our 20-point service package, Breezy EV warranty, and the free pickup within 25 miles of Livingston all apply the same as a cash sale. Call 936-223-1182.

Do you deliver rent-to-own carts?

Yes — free delivery within 25 miles of Livingston, TX, extended delivery 25–50 miles ($50) and 50–100 miles ($75). We handle every delivery personally, show you how to operate and charge the cart, and leave you with the paperwork and support numbers.

What's the term length — how long until I own it?

Terms typically run 36 to 48 months, depending on the cart and your approval. Shorter terms = larger monthly payment but faster ownership. Longer terms = smaller monthly, more total cost. You pick what fits your budget.

What if I decide I don't want it anymore?

You can return the cart during the rental period without an early-termination penalty — it's one of the biggest reasons buyers pick RTO over a loan. You lose the payments you've already made (same as any rental), but you don't owe a lump sum like you would on a repossessed loan.
